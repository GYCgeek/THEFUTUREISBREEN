The Global Youth Coalition has run training programmes for young creators since COP27.

Reeforesting 4 Peace (R4P) trains young people in filmmaking and social media. It was developed with experts from organisations such as NatGeo, Netflix and Walt Disney.

R4P links nature-based solutions with storytelling. It prepares a new generation of creators to tell the story of climate change and the solutions to it.

Through seven free online sessions, participants will learn from industry experts who have worked with some of the biggest names in media. From mastering the art of storytelling to leveraging the power of social media for positive change, this program covers it all.

But what sets this program apart is its focus on impact. It's about empowering young people to become agents of change in their communities and beyond. It's about planting seeds of hope in a world that desperately needs it.

If you want to put your creativity to work for a real difference in the world, we invite you to join us. Let's harness the power of filmmaking and social media to create a better world for future generations. Together, we can change the world – one story at a time.
