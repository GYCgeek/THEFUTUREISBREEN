Six routes to the same work.

Every route funds the same programmes. They differ only in fees, paperwork and how far your gift stretches.

1. Card, one-off or monthly

  1. Through Raisely: Give now.
  2. Monthly gifts can be changed, paused or cancelled any time from your receipt email.

2. Bank transfer

  1. No card fees. Transfer directly to GYC using the details in the table below, with the reference "Donation".
  2. Email us so we can send a receipt.

3. Employer matching

  1. Many employers match gifts to registered nonprofits and fund volunteer hours.
  2. Search your company's giving portal for Global Youth Coalition, or ask HR to add us.

A €50 monthly gift becomes €1,200 a year if your employer matches it.

4. Foundations and donor-advised funds

  1. Recommend a grant to Global Youth Coalition.
  2. Email us for our statutes, registration and any documents your fund needs.

5. Partner with Youth House or Hack for the Futures

  1. House Partner €2,500; Founding House Partner by conversation.
  2. Challenge owners, mentors and track sponsors for Hack for the Futures.

6. In-kind support

  1. Compute and software credits, venues, interpretation, legal help, travel.
  2. These keep our programmes free.

Questions

Is my gift tax-deductible?

It depends on where you live. Write to us and we'll tell you what applies before you give.

Where does the money go?

To the programmes priced above: GYCT, COP bursaries, Youth House and Hack for the Futures. Annual reports are on our Reports page; a full spending breakdown is available on request.

Can I change or cancel a monthly gift?

Yes, any time, from the link in your receipt email or by writing to us.

Can I give without money?

Yes: volunteer, mentor at Hack for the Futures, translate our reports, or offer in-kind support. Write to takeaction@theglobalyouth.co.

Who runs GYC?

A youth-led Swiss association with a Chair, a Champs Council and clear decision rules, reviewed every 90 days. See About.
