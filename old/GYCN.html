Who We Are

The Global Youth Coalition is a worldwide network of young changemakers united by a shared vision for a sustainable future.

Operating across 178 countries, we empower youth to transform climate challenges into opportunities for innovation and leadership. Our community brings together diverse perspectives, skills, and experiences to create meaningful impact where it matters most.

Through collaborative projects, capacity building, and advocacy, we're not just talking about change—we're making it happen.

Ready to make a difference?

Join thousands of young people from around the world who are taking action for a sustainable future.

Maria Silva

Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

"After joining the Global Youth Coalition's reforestation program, I was able to mobilize over 200 young people in my community to plant 5,000 native trees in degraded areas of the Atlantic Forest. What started as a small local initiative has now grown into a regional movement with support from local government and businesses."

"The training and network I gained through GYC gave me the confidence and resources to turn my passion into real impact. Now I'm mentoring other young environmental leaders in Brazil to start their own projects."

Ahmed Hassan

Cairo, Egypt

"Water scarcity has always been a critical issue in my community. Through the Global Youth Climate Training program, I developed the skills to create a water conservation initiative that combines traditional knowledge with modern technology."

"Our project has reduced water consumption by 30% in participating households and has been recognized by the Ministry of Environment. The GYC platform connected me with experts and funding opportunities that made this possible."

Priya Sharma

Mumbai, India
