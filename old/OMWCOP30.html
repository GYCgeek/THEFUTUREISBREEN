It happened.

In the month before COP30, The Trident sailed the Tapajós River, a tributary of the Amazon, through the territories of the Tupinambá and other communities, carrying their voices to COP30. It was GYC's first sailing expedition: more than three weeks on the river, not a crossing of the Atlantic. During COP30 in Belém, the boat became one of our bases, shared with our partners at UON.Earth.

Below is the vision we set out with. The ambition became reality.

The Trident is a youth-led, cross-continental sailing journey to COP30 in Brazil—an odyssey for action, collaboration, and climate innovation. This isn't just another climate gathering; it's a vibrant intersection of cultures, ideas, and solutions aboard three sailing vessels powered by vision, courage, and collective responsibility.

Our crew—the "Tridents"—are changemakers from diverse corners of the globe, representing communities often left out of critical dialogues. Together, we're sailing beyond traditional boundaries to deliver fresh perspectives, bold climate solutions, and authentic stories from the frontline, directly into the heart of global negotiations.

Forget standard conferences or petitions—we're navigating ocean miles as a powerful metaphor for bridging climate ambition and tangible results, making every wave count toward real, measurable action.

"Marking 20 years since the Kyoto Protocol came into effect and 10 years since the Paris Agreement was signed. These milestones represent critical turning points in global climate policy—but commitments alone won't save our planet. Now is the time to transition from promises to concrete action.

The trident 20+10 symbolizes our commitment to transforming global goals into genuine, measurable progress. It's not just a reminder of promises made, but a powerful call-to-action: the next decade defines our planet's fate, and we're literally sailing towards the solutions we urgently need.

We sail because climate action needs more than words—it needs visibility, leadership, and courage in motion. Sailing embodies sustainability, resilience, and the courage required to tackle the challenges ahead. Each mile at sea represents our collective commitment to responsible innovation, resilience, and authentic climate action.

Sailing also unites. On board, cultures blend, ideas flow freely, and solutions are crafted by those whose voices are often drowned out by louder tides. By sailing, we symbolize our collective journey toward an inclusive, just, and sustainable future.

Our crew, known as The Tridents, represents a carefully chosen group of passionate young changemakers, advocates, scientists, entrepreneurs, and storytellers. Each member brings unique experiences from diverse communities worldwide—many facing immediate climate threats.

The Tridents aren't here to protest; they're here to propose. They share powerful insights, practical solutions, and authentic experiences directly with global leaders, media, and stakeholders. By funding The Tridents, you're amplifying genuine voices, investing in youth empowerment, and unlocking the immense potential of collaborative innovation.

The Avocado Framework is our structured approach to turning global climate promises into actionable solutions. Built on three strategic pillars—the Trident of climate action:

  • Nature-Based Solutions – Harnessing ecosystems to build climate resilience.
  • Clean & Just Energy Transition – Promoting equitable access to renewable energy.
  • Loss & Damage Fund – Ensuring frontline communities have the resources to recover and thrive.

By supporting the Avocado Framework, partners align their resources, reputation, and expertise with meaningful, measurable, and community-driven climate impact.

"Who manages logistics and safety?"
A professional, experienced sailing and logistics team oversees safety, logistics, and training. Each crew member undergoes rigorous selection, training, and preparation to ensure a safe, impactful journey.

Because waiting isn't an option. Because promises must become actions. Because youth leadership must translate into policy influence. The climate crisis is urgent, but so is the opportunity: be part of something historic.

Join us as we sail beyond promises—transforming global ambition into real-world impact. Together, we can chart a better course for our planet.

Our main supporter: the Union of Nature

The Trident was made possible by the Union of Nature Foundation (UON), our main supporter for this expedition. UON is a member of the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) that brings nature-protection organisations together around one goal: protecting half the planet by 2050. Through UON.Earth, anyone can protect nature square metre by square metre, with geocoded proof of protection. During COP30, the boat became a shared base for GYC and UON in Belém.

What the partnership left behind

  • Yachana Foundation joins UON. Through the work of GYC founder Agustín Ocaña, UON accepted Ecuador's Yachana Foundation into its network. Yachana has worked in the Ecuadorian Amazon since 1991, combining rainforest conservation with education for Indigenous and mestizo youth.
  • More than 1,000 m² protected with students. A project with Colegio Johannes Kepler in Quito, Ecuador, led personally by Agustín, already protects more than 1,000 m² through UON.Earth.
