GYC's Vision

At GYC, we don't follow tech hype. We use modern tools to fund youth climate missions, empower changemakers, and ensure transparent, verifiable impact.

Our approach is rooted in practicality and purpose. We've carefully selected blockchain technology not because it's trendy, but because it offers unique solutions to real challenges in global youth activism:

  • Transparent funding that crosses borders
  • Verifiable impact tracking
  • Direct support for youth-led initiatives
  • Community ownership of environmental solutions

gyc.gemfunder.com is our platform to mint ethical gems that unlock real-world action. Users can launch campaigns, raise funds, fractionalize ownership, and track mission success.

Powered by gemfunder.com and optimized for youth, our platform transforms digital tokens into tangible impact:

  • Launch climate action campaigns
  • Raise funds transparently
  • Track mission progress in real-time
  • Build a global community of supporters
  • Verify impact with on-chain evidence

Each gem represents not just a digital asset, but a stake in real environmental and social change led by young people worldwide.

Is Blockchain Bad for the Planet?

Not here. We use Proof-of-Stake chains like BNB and Ethereum 2.0 — 99.9% less energy than Bitcoin.

Compared to YouTube or online banking, our energy use is negligible. Plus, our funded missions reforest, clean oceans, and train youth in climate action — real-world regenerative work that offsets our minimal digital footprint many times over.

We've done the math:

  • A single Proof-of-Stake transaction uses less energy than sending 2-3 emails
  • Our platform's annual carbon footprint is offset by just a few trees planted through our Reforesting4Peace initiative
  • The environmental benefits of our funded projects exceed our tech footprint by orders of magnitude

We believe in using the best tools available to drive positive change, while ensuring those tools themselves align with our environmental values.
