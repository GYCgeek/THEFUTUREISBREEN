About Us

WE STARTED IN COP27 TO EMPOWER AND MOBILIZE YOUNG PEOPLE FROM AROUND THE WORLD TO TAKE ACTION ON CLIMATE CHANGE AND PEACE.

In partnership with young professionals and activists across the globe, we have gathered together to tackle the problems with well-planned actions. Our goal: to act. Over the years, not only have our members been making an impact, but also leading and guiding the differences made in their communities. The dream team! Each member has their own realities and gifts. Geeks, policy-makers, writers, scientists, chefs, DJs, musicians, doctors, lawyers—we are the resilient generation of the internet. Tech is our best friend and Earth is our source of energy.

READ MORE >>

OUR VISION

Our vision is a generation of young people with the skills, networks and standing to build the blue-green economy, not just ask for it. GYC is an innovation-driven, youth-led coalition that turns ambition into capability. Through training in green jobs, governance and nature-based solutions, hackathons, entrepreneurship and planetary wellness, we prepare young people to design solutions, lead ventures and take their place wherever decisions are made.

We were the ocean previously, now we are the sky, then we will be the roots, and then we will become fire. Fire after Sky is influence.

READ MORE >>

OUR MISSION

The mission of the Global Youth Coalition is to build capacities, create networks, and foster collaboration. We aim to create a powerful youth-led movement that can advocate for ambitious climate policies, secure climate finance for projects and solutions, and take active participation at the tables where decisions are made, as well as in the businesses that are fueling the change we see in our vision, while ensuring the safety and well-being of our youth members.

Foresight Research

INTERGENERATIONAL EQUITY

A brief introduction to the Global Youth Coalition's foresight research on intergenerational equity (IE), fairness (IGG), and justice (IJ) while the full team gallery is still being assembled.

About the project

Youth-led research for long-term decision making

This collaborative youth-led project explores how young people around the world define and perceive intergenerational equity (IE), fairness (IGG), and justice (IJ). The goal is to co-create future-oriented, youth-informed metrics that help researchers, institutions, and policymakers embed long-term thinking into policy and practice.

  • Examines how intergenerational equity is understood across regions and lived realities.
  • Uses strategic foresight to connect present decisions with future consequences.
  • Translates youth perspectives into practical metrics and policy guidance.
Explore the Full Research Page

Building youth-responsive indicators that can guide policy, research, and accountability.
