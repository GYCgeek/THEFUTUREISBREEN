Privacy notice

How we handle partner inquiries and youth applications on this site.

Version: v0 · HTML5 site · update when CRM / hosting goes live

1. Controller

Global Youth Coalition (“GYC”, “we”) operates GYC House pages. Contact routes are published on theglobalyouth.co.

2. What we collect

  • Partner form: organisation, name, role, email, moment interest, package interest, optional budget band, message, consent flags.
  • Apply form: name, email, country, age band, role, affiliation, moment interest, motivation, optional links and access needs, equity support preference, consent flags.

3. v0 technical note

Until a server-side CRM is connected, form submissions are stored in your browser (localStorage key gyc-house-submissions) so the product flow can be tested. That is not a production data pipeline. Do not treat localStorage as a secure archive. Production will use a controlled backend (e.g. form endpoint → CRM / sheet) with access controls.

4. Purposes

  • Respond to partner inquiries and schedule conversations
  • Review youth applications and send prep materials if selected
  • Operate events safely (access needs, safeguarding)
  • Improve the House programme (aggregated, non-identifying where possible)

5. Legal bases (where GDPR applies)

Consent (forms) and legitimate interests in operating convening programmes and partnerships, balanced against your rights. You may withdraw consent for optional marketing; operational event communication may still be required if you are selected to attend.

6. Retention

Production retention periods will be documented at launch (expected: inquiry data for the active season + limited archive for impact reporting). You may request deletion subject to legal obligations.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited fields with session co-hosts only as needed to run a confirmed programme, under confidentiality expectations.

8. Your rights

Depending on jurisdiction: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability. Use coalition contact channels to exercise rights.

9. Cookies

This static v0 site does not set analytics cookies by default. If analytics are added later, we will update this notice and provide a choice where required.
