Code of conduct

Non-negotiable standards for GYC House spaces — physical and digital.

Version: v0 draft for site launch · subject to Legal Affairs review

1. Purpose

GYC House convenes youth and institutions to work on climate and related global challenges. Everyone deserves safety, dignity, and the ability to participate fully.

2. Who this covers

Partners, speakers, delegates, staff, volunteers, vendors, and guests at GYC House events and related online spaces.

3. Expected behaviour

  • Treat people with respect; disagree with ideas, not identities.
  • No harassment, discrimination, or sexualised behaviour.
  • No youth-washing: do not use young people as props for branding.
  • Honour consent for photos, recording, and attribution.
  • Follow venue rules, accessibility requests, and facilitator instructions.
  • Disclose conflicts of interest when pitching finance or policy asks.

4. Prohibited conduct

  • Harassment, stalking, intimidation, or threats
  • Hate speech or demeaning stereotypes
  • Unwanted sexual attention or contact
  • Retaliation against people who report concerns
  • Deliberate disruption that prevents others from participating

5. Youth safeguarding

Additional care applies where participants are under 18. Adults in positions of power (speakers, partners, staff) must not initiate private one-to-one contact that bypasses official channels. Guardian consent may be required for under-16 applications.

6. Reporting

Report concerns to on-site GYC staff or via the coalition contact channels published for the event. Legal Affairs & Human Rights can be engaged for sensitive cases. We take reports seriously and protect privacy to the extent possible.

7. Consequences

GYC House may warn, re-seat, remove, or permanently ban participants, and may cancel partner sessions that violate this code — without refund of in-kind or cash contributions where misconduct is established.

8. Partner sessions

Hosting a session means accepting this code, using agreed formats, and including youth voices as contributors — not tokens. Pure photo-op panels may be declined or redesigned.

Questions: use the partner inquiry form or coalition contact routes on theglobalyouth.co.
