🇺🇳 Live from Bonn — SB64

Youth AI Listening Lab is running at the Bonn Climate Change Conference

GYC AI Labs is present at SB64 (June 15–26) with a facilitated workshop that puts a structured question to youth delegates: examine the infrastructure. Participants use a custom-built session platform — designed and deployed by our team — to name AI infrastructure signals, classify them as challenges, risks, or opportunities, and vote on the room's priorities. The outputs feed a conference paper that will be submitted to the UNFCCC Technology Mechanism.

The Listening Lab runs in two modules:

Module 1 — AI infrastructure and climate change. Participants identify infrastructure signals (energy, water, chips, governance, information integrity) and articulate what should change, be governed, protected, or scaled.

Module 2 — Youth coordination tools. What would a purpose-built platform for youth climate coordination look like, if WhatsApp was not the default?

A townhall to present the Listening Lab's findings will be announced during the second week of SB64. If you are in Bonn, come find us.

Signals of Change

What is moving in the intergenerational fairness, youth climate, and AI-for-good space right now.

SB64 opens in Bonn — AI on the agenda for the first time at a UNFCCC intersessional

The 64th session of the UNFCCC Subsidiary Bodies opened today in Bonn. For the first time at an intersessional, AI and digital infrastructure are appearing across multiple agenda items — from the Technology Mechanism's #AI4ClimateAction workplan to discussions on information integrity and the digital divide. GYC AI Labs is in the room, running the Listening Lab. The timing of our presence here — the same week the cohort forms for the London hackathon — was not planned this way, but it could not have been better aligned.

UNESCO and Brazil launch Global Initiative for Information Integrity on Climate Change

Following the G20 launch in Rio and the COP30 Declaration, the operational phase of the Global Initiative for Information Integrity on Climate Change is now underway, with UNESCO leading research and action against climate disinformation. This directly connects to the Listening Lab's Module 1 — information integrity is one of the 14 infrastructure themes participants can tag their signals against. The question the Lab puts to youth is not theoretical; the governance infrastructure to address it is being built right now.

UK youth unemployment: NEETs surpass 1 million — Milburn review published

The Milburn review's interim report confirms more than 1M young people in the UK are not in education, employment, or training — a 9% increase in a year, with an estimated £125B annual economic cost. This is the backdrop for every conversation about youth economic participation during London Climate Action Week. The hackathon's Youth & ACE track was designed for this intersection.

Climate Democracy Accelerator — deadline June 26

A 6-month accelerator ($10K–$25K) for organisations at the intersection of climate action and participatory democracy. Deadline June 26. If you know a community group working on youth climate civic-tech — forward this their way.

Moonshot Awards 2026 — AI for Good category, deadline July 12

$5K equity-free grant + 2-year acceleration for changemakers aged 15–30 building early-stage AI with social impact. Dedicated "AI for Good" category. If you are building something at the intersection of AI and climate — this is worth 30 minutes of your time.

Anthropic Mythos-class AI now publicly available

The "straitjacketed" public release of Anthropic's most advanced model means the same underlying capability that shook Wall Street two months ago is now accessible to anyone building. For hackathon participants using Anthropic credits, the tools available in one day of building just got meaningfully more powerful. The question becomes: what can a room of 60 people, with Mythos-class infrastructure and a shared climate problem, create in 8 hours?
