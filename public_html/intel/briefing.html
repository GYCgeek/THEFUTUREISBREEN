      Track record

      Use this to judge whether the platform earns trust — public, dated predictions and the human-review record, kept honest over time.

      Open predictions

      Falsifiable calls with a due date and a stated confidence, made before the outcome is known. They resolve to hit or miss on their date — no retroactive editing. This is the accountability record; calibration (are we right as often as we claim?) sharpens as they resolve.

      Analyst review activity

      Where a human has checked or corrected the AI's provisional scoring. Every override is recorded in the append-only review journal.

      Triage radar

      Use this when triaging by impact vs uncertainty — act, prepare, monitor, or leave in the background. This is a radar, not a scenario 2×2.

      Scores cluster; treat quadrant as the unit of action, not the pixel.

      Every signal on one impact × uncertainty map — impact rises upward, uncertainty grows to the right (lower credibility plus higher novelty), and the median splits carve four action quadrants. Bubble colour marks the dominant domain; numbered bubbles are signals sharing an identical score cell. Click a quadrant to expand it into a zoomed map with a domain breakdown and the ranked signal list; single bubbles open the signal card directly.

      Signal globe

      Use this when geography and regional precedent matter — drag to rotate, click markers for country-level signal lists.

      Geographic footprint of the knowledge base. Drag to rotate, click a marker or a country in the list to see its signals. Marker size = signal count; red markers contain high-priority signals; hollow teal rings mark countries mentioned in signal text (sized by mention count). Click one to see what cites it. Signals tagged “International” are listed separately because they belong to no single country.
