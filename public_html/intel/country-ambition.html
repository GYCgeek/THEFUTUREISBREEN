How This Analysis Works

Each country is researched against six dimensions and scored mechanically from the structured legal file that powers the dashboard. This is an intelligence product, not formal legal advice.

1. Constitutional Recognition
0-3 points
Constitutional text or doctrine protecting future generations, posterity, or long-term environmental rights.
2. Dedicated Legislation
0-4 points
Statutes directly or indirectly embedding intergenerational protection in durable legal form.
3. Institutional Mechanisms
0-3 points
Commissioners, tribunals, authorities, committees, or similar bodies with future-facing mandates.
4. Judicial Enforcement
0-2 points
Court rulings that recognize or enforce duties relevant to future generations.
5. International Leadership
0-2 points
Treaty participation, declarations, and other international commitments with long-term equity dimensions.
6. Policy Integration
0-2 points
Impact assessment, planning, budgeting, or climate-governance tools that carry intergenerational consequences.
