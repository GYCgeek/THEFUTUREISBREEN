How governments are supposed to look before they leap

This section inventories the machinery — impact assessments, commissioners, long-term budgets — rather than the news. The first live family is Intergenerational Impact Assessments. Completing a form is not the same as changing a decision.

Subsection · IIAs

Intergenerational Impact Assessments

An Intergenerational Impact Assessment is a procedure, not a slogan: someone has to assess effects on young people, future cohorts, or the unborn before a law, budget, plan, or project is adopted, with some legal or administrative force.

Completing an IA is common. Changing a decision is rare. Flanders JoKER is the only instrument that can stall a dossier — and only when the report is missing.

Not an IIA

  • A constitutional future-generations clause by itself (Germany Art. 20a, Italy Art. 9)
  • A commissioner or ombuds without a pre-decision annex
  • A 40-year fiscal outlook or intergenerational report
  • A completed form that left the decision unchanged

Country dossiers

enacted Italy Constitutional Art. 9 + statutory VIG. Implementing DPCMs unpublished; 2026 government AIR still omits VIG.

Italy's intergenerational equity model is a three-layer stack: (1) constitutional entrenchment via Constitutional Law 1/2022 (Articles 9 and 41), naming future generations and environmental limits on economic activity; (2) administrative operationalisation through Generational Impact Assessment (VIG) in Law 167/2025, integrated into regulatory impact analysis for government bills; and (3) judicial enforcement through Constitutional Court doctrine on equità intergenerazionale, spanning public finance (from 2019) and environment (especially post-2022). Italy does not have a Wales-style Future Generations Commissioner, but it is among the few states that constitutionally name future generations alongside environment and biodiversity. As of mid-2026, VIG is legally binding but the implementing DPCM and Observatory are still being stood up, while municipalities (Parma, Bologna) already run local VIG ahead of central government practice.

  • Layer 1 · Constitutional foundation
    Constitutional Law No. 1 of 11 February 2022 — Binds legislature, executive, and courts; elevates future generations from policy language to fundamental principles.
  • Layer 2 · Statutory operationalisation
    Law No. 167 of 10 November 2025 (Art. 4–5) — VIG in AIR for government normative acts; National Observatory at Presidency of the Council of Ministers.
  • Layer 3 · Judicial doctrine
    Constitutional Court jurisprudence (2019–2026) — Equità intergenerazionale as legitimacy parameter in fiscal discipline and environmental derogations.

enacted Netherlands Administrative generatietoets is live as method. Budget skip on MN 2026. One consultation-stage Kinderrechtentoets amendment.

The Netherlands is one of two countries GYC classifies as having an enacted national Intergenerational Impact Assessment — but the instrument is administrative, not a veto. The Beleidskompas generatietoets asks ministries to test irreversible or redistributive effects on current and future generations before a decision; nobody can stall a dossier for a missing toets. A cousin tool, the Kinderrechtentoets, is a living-child CRIA now instructed for jeugdzorg bills. Bite is thin: Miljoenennota 2026 skipped the Senate's generatietoets motion; the only documented amendment is two consultation-stage safeguards on Wet reikwijdte Jeugdwet. Next public test is Prinsjesdag 15 September 2026.

  • Layer 1 · Administrative IIA (enacted method)
    Beleidskompas step 4 + Leidraad Toekomstgericht Beleid generatietoets — Ministries should run a generatietoets when effects may redistribute across generations, are irreversible, or lock later cohorts in. Hulpmiddel, not a statutory veto.
  • Layer 2 · Child-rights cousin (youth-care bills)
    Kinderrechtentoets — Senate motion 36.546, I (7 Oct 2025) — Four-step CRC assessment on future jeugdzorg bills, recorded in the memorie van toelichting. Kinderombudsman advises; cannot void a bill.
  • Layer 3 · Climate duty (not IIA review)
    Urgenda (Hoge Raad 2019) via ECHR Arts. 2 and 8 — Intergenerational climate duty of care. Does not require a pre-decision generatietoets and does not review a missing one.

cousin Germany Art. 20a names future generations. Jugend-Check is the densest published youth-IA series — and the Bundestag is not bound.

Germany has a constitutional future-generations clause (Basic Law Article 20a) and the densest published youth-impact series in the GYC corpus: KomJC screened 490 federal bills in the 20th Bundestag and published 91 Jugend-Checks. The check is not an Intergenerational Impact Assessment of unborn cohorts. It assesses living 12–27-year-olds; the Bundestag is not bound to amend; who can block: nobody. Documented bite is skip (minijobs 2022 dropped two youth-protective clauses after the check) or check-then-enact (Thuringia court-organisation statute kept the draft robe clause). Neubauer 2021 is intergenerational climate doctrine, not Jugend-Check review.

  • Layer 1 · Constitutional foundation
    Basic Law Article 20a (1994) — Names future generations as the reason the state must protect the natural foundations of life. No commissioner and no statutory IIA annex.
  • Layer 2 · Youth-check (administrative, published)
    KomJC Jugend-Check (since 2017) — Independent scientific pre-screen of every federal government draft for effects on ages 12–27. Published. Bundestag not bound.
  • Layer 3 · Climate freedom over time
    BVerfG Neubauer (24 March 2021) — Offloading a radical post-2030 mitigation burden onto later generations threatens future freedom. Climate law, not Jugend-Check procedure.

cousin Belgium Flanders JoKER only. Unique stall gate for a missing report. No federal IIA.

Belgium has no federal Intergenerational Impact Assessment. The live instrument is Flemish: Jeugddecreet Article 7 JoKER (kind- en jongereneffectrapport) on Flemish Government draft decrees that directly affect children and young people, now through age 30. It is the only IIA-family tool in the GYC corpus with documented dossier-blocking bite — the Council of State required a missing JoKER in opinion 69.100/1 (18 May 2021). Completing JoKER unblocks the file; it does not force a rewrite. After the 13 March 2026 age-30 expansion, bill 864 filed a completed JoKER and the Council did not stall it.

  • Layer 1 · Flemish statutory JoKER
    Jeugddecreet Article 7 — kind- en jongereneffectrapport — Mandatory annex on Flemish Government ontwerpdecreten that directly affect children and young people: effects with the measure, without it, and alternatives to avoid, limit or remedy serious harm.
  • Layer 2 · Council of State stall gate
    Raad van State / Conseil d'État legislation section — Can require a missing JoKER before the dossier proceeds. Completing the report unblocks; no merits veto on a completed JoKER.
  • Layer 3 · Federal and Walloon gap
    None enacted in the GYC corpus as of 17 August 2026 — No federal IIA, no Walloon equivalent located, no Future Generations Commissioner.

cousin Austria Mandatory WFA child/youth dimension since 2013. Budgetbegleitgesetz 2025 completed the check then enacted the freeze.

WFA-Kinder-und-Jugend-Verordnung (BGBl. II Nr. 495/2012) requires simplified screening then a deeper assessment in material cases, travelling with the government bill. The assessor is the bill author. Budgetbegleitgesetz 2025 (BGBl. I Nr. 25/2025) quantified a two-year Familienbeihilfe freeze for two million children and a school-travel copay rise, and enacted both.

cousin France 2016 youth-impact clause requires intergenerational-justice analysis in government bills. No proven rewrite located.

Circular 5857-SG (2 May 2016) adds a clause d'impact jeunesse to études d'impact, covering equality among young people, intergenerational justice and non-discrimination, with compensating measures expected if a text would penalise current or future young cohorts. Loi 2025-268 filled the youth-impact sections; bite versus adopted articles is unknown.
