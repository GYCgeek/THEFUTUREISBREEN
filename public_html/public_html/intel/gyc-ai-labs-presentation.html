Platform

Foresight Intelligence

A live, static-site intelligence dashboard. No backend, no database servers — just generated HTML, JSON, and embedded JavaScript, deployed over FTP.

Signal database

1,322 signals from policy, litigation, economics, technology, and social movements. Each signal has a source, summary, confidence score, country, and intergenerational lens classification.

Trend detection

8 active trends tracked weekly — intergenerational fiscal rebalancing, youth climate litigation, future generations institutions, youth digital protection, and more. Weekly momentum charts.

Foresight views

Three Horizons map, impact-uncertainty matrix, futures wheels, projection strips, counter-signals. Built-in foresight methodology, not just a database.

Workshop packs

Exportable workshop kits for each major trend — S-curve analysis, evidence packs, implication maps, four futures scenarios, decision moments.

Live

Dashboard features

The live briefing at theglobalyouth.co/public_html/intel/ includes:

Weekly briefing

New signals, re-sighted signals, trend momentum, futures wheels, review queue, high-priority watchlist, going-stale signals.

All signals view

Filter by domain chip (10 categories) + topic dropdown. Search, sort by score/date/country. Thematic synthesis pages per topic.

Three Horizons map

Signals plotted by time horizon (H1 present, H2 transition, H3 transformation). Dot colour = domain, size = corroboration, ring = high priority.

Impact-uncertainty matrix

Every signal on one map. Four quadrants for action prioritisation. Click to drill into domain breakdown and ranked signal list.

Country analysis

Country ambition index, similarity analysis, gap analysis. 80+ country profiles with signal counts and policy area coverage.

Finance graph

Knowledge graph of funders, dimensions, and signals. 68 funders, 14 finance dimensions, $19B+ tracked. Force-directed canvas.

Trends

8 active trends

Each trend is tracked weekly with signal counts, country spread, and momentum direction.

Intergenerational equity mainstreaming

711 signals · 75 countries · accelerating. The core trend: intergenerational equity language migrating from advocacy into budgets, courts, and policy.

Youth digital protection

219 signals · 53 countries · accelerating. Online safety regulation, platform accountability, digital rights for minors.

Intergenerational fiscal rebalancing

495 signals · 68 countries · accelerating. Tax reform, pension restructuring, sovereign wealth, baby bonds, debt-for-future swaps.

Youth climate litigation

169 signals · 34 countries · accelerating. Strategic climate lawsuits by and for young people, from Montana to the ICJ.

Future generations institutions

92 signals · 33 countries · accelerating. Commissioners, ombudspersons, parliamentary committees for future generations.

Youth participation governance

271 signals · 66 countries · accelerating. Youth councils, advisory bodies, voting age reform, participatory budgeting.

See where your work fits in the global picture. Find peer organisations, track shared trends, and use the platform for advocacy evidence.
