How the project works

From evidence of change to decisions.

The platform scans broadly, connects related evidence, interprets possible implications, and prepares decision-ready outputs. The machinery remains inspectable, but the front door starts with the conclusion.

Foresight, not prediction.

We explore multiple plausible implications and keep uncertainty visible. Analyst judgment can confirm, correct, or override automated assessment.
