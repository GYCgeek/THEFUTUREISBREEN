How this is built. The dimension coverage is computed live over the tracked signals (signals-v3.json) using the same 15 keyword definitions as the weekly finance-scanner-weekly.mjs cron (shared via lib/finance-dimensions.mjs, so the cron and this page never drift). A dimension is covered at 3+ signals, thin at 1–2, a gap at 0. The funding pipeline is parsed from the daily funding scans (parse-funding.mjs); "tracked finance" there is what's been surfaced, not money awarded, and large multi-grant fund pools are flagged.
