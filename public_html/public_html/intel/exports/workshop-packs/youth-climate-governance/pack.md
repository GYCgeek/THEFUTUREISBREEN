## 5. Four futures

> **Projection may be outdated**: trend gained 97 signals since authoring

### no change — Voice without leverage

Youth climate delegations attend, speak and are thanked, while the decisions that matter are taken without binding regard to their input. Access grows but leverage over outcomes does not.

### marginal — Formalised inclusion

Youth constituencies gain more standing places in climate processes and occasional negotiating roles. Their fingerprints appear on some texts, though the core bargaining still happens among states and blocs.

### adaptive — Governed-in youth

Youth roles become embedded features of climate governance — funded delegations, guaranteed submissions, accountability mechanisms — that persist across summits rather than being renegotiated each year.

### radical — Rights-anchored authority

Youth climate participation fuses with rights-based obligations, giving young people enforceable standing to shape and challenge climate decisions. Participation becomes a duty owed to them, not a courtesy extended.

**What to watch:**
