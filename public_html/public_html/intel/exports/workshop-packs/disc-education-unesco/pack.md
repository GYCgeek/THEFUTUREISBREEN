## 5. Four futures

### no change — Framework on a shelf

Futures-education and UNESCO framing circulates in conferences and guidance but does not reach curricula or classrooms at scale. It stays a specialist vocabulary rather than a change in what students learn.

### marginal — Pilot uptake

A cluster of ministries and networks pilot futures literacy and anticipatory education. Adoption is enthusiast-driven and uneven, but early evidence and shared materials begin to accumulate.

### adaptive — Into the curriculum

Futures thinking is written into national curricula and teacher training in several systems, backed by assessment and funding, moving from pilot to standard practice in those jurisdictions.

### radical — Anticipatory schooling

Anticipation and long-term thinking become organising principles of mainstream education, reshaping curricula, pedagogy and how systems judge success — a structural shift in what schooling is for.

**What to watch:**
- (adaptive) A national curriculum embeds futures literacy with assessment and funding
- (radical) Multiple systems restructure curricula around anticipatory learning
- (no_change) Pilots end without institutionalisation when grants expire
