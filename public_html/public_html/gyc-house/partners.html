Partner with GYC House

Host a session, fund equity seats, or anchor a Builder Lab track. Benefits are public. Pricing is by conversation — we reply within 5 business days.

Ethos

Partnership, not photo-ops

Meaningful progress happens when institutions and youth practitioners co-create in the same room — with preparation before, and continuation after. We decline pure greenwashing panels.

What you get

  • Curated room mix: youth + policy + finance + builders
  • Format kit (roundtable, fireside, workshop, demo…)
  • Facilitation support and session design guardrails
  • Prep brief for speakers; impact log for reporting

What we ask

  • Cash, venue, seats, mentors, or compute — named clearly
  • Respect for our code of conduct and youth safeguarding
  • A real outcome intent (not only brand placement)
  • Willingness to log continuations post-event

Packages

Four starting points

Mix-and-match is fine. Tell us your goal; we map the right tier.

House Anchor

Own a day, track, or House block at a flagship moment.

  • Naming on track / day
  • 1–2 hosted sessions
  • Seat pack for your team
  • Logo + impact report feature
  • Partner prep brief

Session Host

One curated format with the right people in the room.

  • Format design support
  • Room + tech baseline
  • Youth mix in the guest list
  • Promotion in House programme

Builder Sponsor

Power a Lab track (e.g. Hack for the Futures at LCAW).

  • Track sponsorship & prizes
  • Mentor / judge seats
  • Hiring & pipeline access to prototypes
  • Compute / credits welcome as in-kind

Access Ally

Fund youth travel, accreditation, and equity seats.

  • Highest-trust logo placement
  • Named equity cohort
  • Story rights for impact reporting
  • Direct line to GYC Legal Affairs on safeguarding norms
