Scenario matrix, impact × uncertainty

Use this when prioritising by impact vs uncertainty — most reliable after analyst reviews spread the scores.

Most scores are still AI-provisional — treat quadrant placement as directional until more analyst reviews land.

Every signal is placed by potential impact (vertical) and uncertainty (horizontal). Lower credibility plus higher novelty increases uncertainty. Bubble size = signals sharing that exact position; quadrant boundaries sit at the data medians, with per-quadrant counts in the corners. Click a bubble to list its signals. Single-signal bubbles open the full card directly. Differentiation will sharpen as analyst reviews spread the scores; today's AI-flat scoring keeps the map coarse.

Signal globe

Use this when geography and regional precedent matter — drag to rotate, click markers for country-level signal lists.

Geographic footprint of the knowledge base. Drag to rotate, click a marker or a country in the list to see its signals. Marker size = signal count; red markers contain high-priority signals; hollow teal rings mark countries mentioned in signal text (sized by mention count). Click one to see what cites it. Signals tagged “International” are listed separately because they belong to no single country.
