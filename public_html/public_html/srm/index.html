What it doesFrom raw sightings to ranked judgment

An automated scanner feeds raw signals into a deterministic Node.js pipeline. Every stage is inspectable, every output is a plain file, and a scripted verification suite runs after every build.

1 · Scan

A scheduled scanner collects developments in solar radiation modification, geoengineering governance, outdoor experiments, country positions and the scientific literature into a raw signals database.

2 · Fuse

Migration merges duplicates, fixes encodings, links sightings, and classifies every signal by domain, horizon, maturity and the SRM governance framework (research governance, moratoria, deployment, oversight).

3 · Judge

Signals are scored on six dimensions (credibility, novelty, relevance, impact, urgency, geography). LLM-assessed scores with written rationales replace rule heuristics as coverage grows; analyst review overrides both.

4 · Act

Outputs: an offline dashboard, a weekly diff digest led by upcoming decision moments, advocacy triggers with pre-agreed playbooks, and markdown briefs ready to paste into position notes.

How it helps an organizationBuilt to close the insight-to-decision gap

📄Decision artifacts

One command exports a litigation thread, a trend dossier or the top-15 watchlist as clean markdown. That creates briefing packs for a legal working group, a board meeting or a funder call in seconds.

📈Evidence for funders

🌍Geography that's real

Honest limitationsWhat this system is not

An intelligence tool that oversells itself produces worse decisions than no tool. Read these before trusting any number on the dashboard.

  • Most judgment is still provisional.0 of 47 signals are analyst-reviewed and 0 carry LLM-assessed scores. The remaining 47 are scored by keyword heuristics that cluster heavily. Treat rankings as triage.
  • Coverage is biased toward English-language, EU and institutional sources.One scanner, largely official feeds. Only 0 of 47 signals are multi-source, so corroboration is weak and entire regions are under-observed. Absence of a signal is not evidence of absence.
  • Threads are hand-curated.The 0 story threads are defined in code. A discovery engine now proposes emergent trend candidates for analyst confirmation, but it is keyword-based. It surfaces patterns, a human still decides which are real, and thread detection remains manual.
  • Matching is lexical.The SRM-governance lens (44/47 tagged), country mentions and the dashboard chatbot all run on word-boundary regex. They miss paraphrase and non-English text, and the chatbot answers only what keyword search can reach.
  • No track record yet.Scores are judgments. The system does not yet record whether what it flagged actually materialized, so there is no calibration evidence behind the priority bands.
  • Single-analyst, local-first.No accounts, no concurrent editing, and no push notifications. The digest file is the alert channel. Calendar dates in decision moments are approximate until manually confirmed.

Potential improvementsThe roadmap, in order of leverage

Each item attacks a limitation above. "Now" means the machinery already exists and only needs use. "Next" is designed but not built. "Later" needs new architecture.

  • Extend LLM scoring to the full knowledge basenowThe enrichment sidecar, validation and fallback logic are live. 0/47 are covered. Each batch of 20 takes one LLM session via enrich-signals.mjs prompt to apply.
  • Work the review queue weeklynowA click-through review console (node scripts/dev-serve.mjs to review.html) or the terminal review --batch converts provisional scores into accountable human judgment in minutes. It also builds the dataset that later recalibrates the AI.
  • Trend & thread discoverynextA weekly job that clusters unassigned or fast-growing topic groups and proposes trend candidates in the digest. That would turn the tracker into a scanner of unknown patterns.
  • More source channelsnextCourt dockets, parliamentary trackers and UN document feeds flowing into the same raw-signal shape would make corroboration a real signal and reduce regional blind spots.
  • Resolution tracking & calibrationnowLive: predictions.mjs logs dated, falsifiable forecasts tied to signals (0 seeded) and computes hit rate + Brier score as they resolve. The digest nudges you to grade them. Keep logging 1 to 2 per high-priority signal to build the track record.
  • Digest delivery & multi-analyst reviewlaterEmail or WhatsApp delivery of the weekly digest, and a review journal that supports several named reviewers with disagreement surfaced clearly.
