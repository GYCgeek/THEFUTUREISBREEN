Three ways the House shows up

One brand. Three formats. Partners buy a clear product — not a vague “panel.”

Houses

Multi-day hubs at global moments: sessions morning to night, partner-hosted formats, youth cohort, media capture.

  • Roundtables, firesides, panels

  • Equity seats + prep packs

  • Continuation intros logged

How a day works

Morning to night — with a spine

09:00
Open brief & orientation Youth cohort + partners align on the day’s tracks
10:30
Roundtables & workshops Climate finance · policy · adaptation · AI systems
13:00
Working lunch / fireside Intergenerational exchange with decision-makers
15:00
Builder Lab blocks or demo hour Prototypes, mentoring, public panels
